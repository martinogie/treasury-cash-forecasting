Which forecasting method to trust

MethodDaily MAPE inDaily MAPE outWeekly MAPE inWeekly MAPE outWeek-13 balance error

Daily MAPE covers the first two weeks of each forecast; weekly MAPE covers all 13 weeks. Balance error is the mean absolute miss on cumulative net cash by week 13.

  • Seasonal naive (same weekday last week) breaks on every holiday and month end.
  • Holt-Winters follows the level but cannot see payroll, tax dates or holiday pile-ups.
  • Gradient boosting on total inflows and outflows learns the calendar and gets both totals within about 7%, but its inflow and outflow errors do not cancel, so the balance drifts.
  • Driver-based forecasts card volume once, then derives receipts, payouts and chargebacks from it using the settlement rules. A volume miss moves both sides together, which is why its balance error is a fraction of the others'.

How it works

  • Card volume is forecast by calendar day with gradient boosting on the ratio to its trailing 28-day level, so growth and seasonality (paydays, holidays, the November–December peak) carry through.
  • Volume settles T+1 on business days, so weekend and holiday volume piles onto the next business day. Daily merchants are paid a day after settlement; weekly merchants are paid in a Friday batch.
  • Payroll, vendor runs and estimated taxes come from the treasury calendar; interest uses last month's amount; unplanned payments are not forecast.
  • The P10–P90 band is σ·√(business days ahead), with σ fitted on backtest errors of cumulative net cash.

All data is synthetic, generated with known settlement rules so each method can be checked against the truth. Built with AI-assisted coding (Claude); modeling choices, validation and review are my own.
